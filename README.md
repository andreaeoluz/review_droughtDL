# Deep Learning Approaches for Drought Forecasting, Monitoring and Impact Assessment: A Comprehensive Systematic Review

This repository contains the data and supplementary materials for the systematic review published in *Applied Soft Computing*: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1568494626006447

> Luz, A. E. O., Negri, R. G., Santos, L. B. L. (2026). Deep learning approaches for drought forecasting, monitoring and impact assessment: A comprehensive systematic review. *Applied Soft Computing*, 197, 115196. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2026.115196

# 🌍 Overview

Droughts are complex and multifaceted hazards with severe socio-environmental consequences. This study systematically reviews the application of deep learning (DL) models in drought prediction and monitoring, with a particular focus on the integration of remote sensing (RS) data.

# 🔎 Search Strategy

Advanced searches were run in Scopus (titles, abstracts and keywords) and Web of Science (topic), restricted to peer-reviewed journal articles in English published between January 2015 and July 2025. The queries below are reported exactly as in Appendix B of the article.

**Scopus**

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY (Deep learning; Drought; Remote sensing)
AND (LIMIT-TO (DOCTYPE, "ar"))
AND (LIMIT-TO (EXACTKEYWORD, "Remote Sensing")
OR LIMIT-TO (EXACTKEYWORD, "Deep Learning")
OR LIMIT-TO (EXACTKEYWORD, "Drought"))
AND (LIMIT-TO LANGUAGE, "English"))
```

**Web of Science**

```text
TS=("deep learning" AND drought AND "remote sensing")
AND LA=English
AND DT=Article
AND PY=2015--2025
```

**Selection.** The searches returned 93 records from Web of Science and 89 from Scopus. After the automated filters and the removal of duplicates, 119 unique articles were screened by title and abstract and then by full text. Studies were included when they applied deep learning with a direct focus on drought monitoring, assessment or prediction, including closely related proxies (soil moisture, vegetation stress, surface water availability, forest mortality) when their relevance to drought was explicit. Studies without a clear link to drought, reviews, retracted publications and works with restricted access were excluded. The final sample has 87 studies:

| Source | Final articles |
|---|---|
| Scopus only | 12 |
| Web of Science only | 19 |
| Both databases | 56 |
| **Total** | **87** |

The records retrieved from each database and the screening decisions are in `data/selection_articles.xlsx`; the coded information of the 87 studies is in `data/data_extraction.xlsx`.

# 📁 Repository Structure

```bash
├── data/
│   ├── selection_articles.xlsx    # Article screening and inclusion/exclusion tracking
│   └── data_extraction.xlsx       # Final metadata table with detailed annotations
├── notebooks/
│   ├── data_representation.ipynb  # Generates Figures 3–14, in the order of the manuscript
│   └── ne_10m_admin_0_countries.zip  # Natural Earth country boundaries (1:10m) for Figure 7
├── figures/                       # Final figures used in the manuscript (Figure1.pdf ... Figure14.pdf)
├── references/                    # BibTeX file with all cited literature
└── README.md                      # Repository documentation
```

Run the notebook from the repository root or from `notebooks/`; each figure is saved as `figures/FigureN.pdf`. Figure 7 reads the Natural Earth *Admin 0 – Countries* shapefile directly from `notebooks/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.zip` (no need to unzip). Figures 1 and 2 are LaTeX diagrams and are not generated by the notebook.
